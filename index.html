My Favourite Cartoon Character

Aang

Aang, the main character of Avatar: The Last Airbender, is my favorite character because he is very powerful funny, and cool person with very amazing stories and fights.

He uses amazing gadgets from the future to solve problems in creative ways.

Character Details

  • Name: Aang
  • Best Friend: Appa
  • Favourite Food: Egg Custart Tart
  • Special Power: Is the Avatar, controls fire, water, earth, and air

Fun Fact: Aang and his flying bison got stuck in an iceberg for one hundred years, and when they came out, they were the same age as they were when they first got stuck.

Aang and his stories teach us about the power of hope and how important it is to not let your past mistakes ruin your future.
